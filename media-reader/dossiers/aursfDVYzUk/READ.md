> **The location half of this channel's entry model: the volume profile as a map of fair and unfair price, and the rule that a setup is only worth looking for at a value-area edge, a point of control or a low-volume node, never in the middle of nowhere. Also the corpus's only figure for how long institutions take to route size: one and a half to two days.**

## In brief

The title promises an entry and the video delivers a map. The map is the
volume profile: volume summed at each price rather than under each candle, so
a day draws a shape with a peak where most business was done. Around the peak
sits the value area, roughly 70% of the day's trades, and he calls that range
fair price. Everything above and below it is unfair.

What makes the map matter is who wants what. The large participants need
balance to do business at all; they sometimes take one and a half to two days
to route an order, and cannot do it in a fast market. So a fast move is not
them shopping, it is someone hedging or getting out, and the thin volume it
leaves is the trace. Hence the rule: look for a trade at the point of control,
the value-area edges and the low-volume nodes, then check with a setup. No
setup is shown. The trade in the title never arrives.

## The argument

He opens with a complaint about traders who place trades all over the market
with different setups, and says flatly that it does not work. The alternative
is to know exactly where counter trades belong, where breakouts belong, where
volume dries up and where a market is getting out of control. The profile
predefines those places; the setup then confirms, in the moment, that what you
need is happening. That is the Idea and Proof of
[NkQeOVDTAec](../NkQeOVDTAec/CARD.md) stated a few months earlier and with the
Proof left out, and it is the Location leg of Carmine Rosato's context,
location, confirmation in [7facFfjQ0UE](../7facFfjQ0UE/CARD.md). Both men
agree that a setup found in the wrong place is not a setup.

The vocabulary is standard and he is careful with it. The value area is where
the market did the majority of its trades, about 70% of the day, or as he later
puts it, 68.2 or 70% of all trades. He separates the two points of control,
the volume-weighted VPOC of the volume profile and the time-weighted point of
control of the market profile, which is the distinction
[K8qtT2_axPo](../K8qtT2_axPo/CARD.md) draws in full and
[1utSjHs_Mq8](../1utSjHs_Mq8/CARD.md) turns into a signal when the two
diverge. The German label diagram on screen is the one
[YmygDgtoxO8](../YmygDgtoxO8/CARD.md) also shows, and the two videos agree
on every term.

Then the story that carries the method. Big participants feel comfortable when
supply and demand are in balance, because that is when the market offers them
fair prices. They do not use profiles, he says; they have to route orders, and
for that they sometimes need one and a half to two days depending on the
instrument. A pension fund with inflows needs time to place them at fair
prices. When prices get out of balance on news, these participants usually
stay out, and the moves that follow are made by those who have to give up
positions or want in quickly. This is the same account of imbalance as
[m3IMdc7QwN4](../m3IMdc7QwN4/CARD.md), where an aggressive push was made by
participants in distress, and as Carmine's opening premise in
[QaNPAaEnB5E](../QaNPAaEnB5E/CARD.md), where an event creates the imbalance
and a new fair value forms around it.

The location rule follows. Day after day of value areas, drawn as boxes, will
overlap, and in the overlaps a counter trade often takes place because the
market tries to return to balance. He calls the point of control a magnet as
long as the environment is stable, and says the market is in stable phases
70% of the time, the base rate this channel repeats in
[K8qtT2_axPo](../K8qtT2_axPo/CARD.md) and
[YmygDgtoxO8](../YmygDgtoxO8/CARD.md). But he immediately asks what additional
information you need to execute the counter trade, since the market can
always break through instead. That is Carmine's position in
[5qBo04SMUFc](../5qBo04SMUFc/CARD.md): a level is not support because it is
drawn, only because the order flow confirms it. The sibling podcast
[RwQBdF9TSvc](../RwQBdF9TSvc/CARD.md) says the same in almost the same words.

The break-through is where the video earns its place in the corpus. Why does a
market that leaves balance move so fast? First answer: everyone who was short
hits the brakes and exits. Second answer, one sentence later: the stories
about stop-loss waves are fairy tales; what actually happens is that large
participants have to hedge, which is what futures are for. Read both
sentences. He concedes the mechanical stop-out and denies the label and the
agency, exactly the shape of the argument in
[RwQBdF9TSvc](../RwQBdF9TSvc/CARD.md), which grants that positions have to be
closed while rejecting the idea that anyone hunts them. It puts the channel
against Carmine's premise in [0QlGCz6U_1g](../0QlGCz6U_1g/CARD.md), where
patient money buys inside the stop hunt, and against the sweep-then-enter
method of Trading Notes in [IUWvHVout94](../IUWvHVout94/CARD.md). The word
retail is never spoken here; the card's phrasing is ours.

The rest is consequence. A fast market cannot build a volume peak, because
nobody can calmly find supply and demand there, so the low-volume node is
the footprint of imbalance and the peak is the footprint of business done.
When price reaches a peak, assume a reaction; whether that is a counter trade
or a place to take profit depends on the idea you came with. The three
questions are where to look, which way the global players are leaning, and
what setup you are looking for, which is the structure-first rule of
[43JaKHRvxHk](../43JaKHRvxHk/CARD.md) in a different order.

He closes with the one setup he rules out: a breakout inside a value area
makes no sense, because you will not get a fast move inside fair price without
news. That is consistent with the channel's own breakout videos,
[uFxYcpiaOpw](../uFxYcpiaOpw/CARD.md) and
[jXR1afMy-3o](../jXR1afMy-3o/CARD.md), which trade the break out of balance,
never within it. What the video never gives is the entry the title sells: no
setup, no stop, no target, no trade.

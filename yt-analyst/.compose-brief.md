# Composer brief — corpus facts as of 2026-09-06

Read this with the skill. Every statement here has been verified against
transcripts during this run; several correct earlier card wordings that you may
still encounter in older cards or in `corpus.json`. **Where a card disagrees
with this file, this file wins and the disagreement is an audit finding.**

## The corpus

Two order-flow spines: **Carmine Rosato**'s 8-episode series, and the German
channel **Tom Vorwald EN / WorldClassEdge** (21 videos). A third school,
**Smart Money Decode X** (10 videos), is ICT/smart-money. Plus Trading Notes,

## Corrected facts — use these wordings

- **Statistics (NkQeOVDTAec).** 200–300 trades before figures mean anything;
  a **worked example** at a 50% hit rate giving an average risk-reward of
  *"maybe a two"*. **Never write "R:R > 2"** — that was a card error, and the
  50% is conditional (*"if you should now… reach a hit rate of 50%"*).
- **Heatmaps.** The disagreement with Carmine's ep. 3 is about **sufficiency,
  not use**. That presenter calls the heatmap *"such a wonderful tool"*,
  demonstrates it on screen and recommends it to beginners; his claim is that
  it is *"not a good way to build an edge"*. Never write "professionals reject
  heatmaps".
- **Delta thresholds.** Carmine is **not** thresholdless. At ep. 3 09:02 he
  disclaims fixed numbers — *"I don't look for specific numbers, I look more so
  for outliers in the data"* — then demonstrates a session baseline: 200–500
  typical, over a thousand an outlier.
- **Size threshold.** *"Over 250–300 contracts"* on ES applies to a **single
  standalone order** — the qualifier is *"isolated and on its own"*.
- **Support and resistance.** The Vorwald podcast **agrees** with Carmine ep. 5
  more than it conflicts: a level *"truly becomes real support and resistance
  because the market confirms it for me"*. The narrow disagreement is point
  versus area, not existence.
- **Stop-hunting.** That podcast rejects **institutional agency**, not the
  mechanical effect — it concedes *"that's a normal principle that positions
  have to be closed accordingly"*. "Inaccurate" and "retail myth" are Gemini's
  words, not his.
- **Iceberg orders.** **Two** independent arrivals (the Vorwald channel and
  Trading Notes), not three. Carmine describes the mechanism as absorption and
  never names it.
- **The one live trade (yWO8hVpRXeY).** A stated risk (23 ticks), a real ATAS
  execution panel and a **locked-in floor** of 74 ticks. **No target is stated
  anywhere**, the "1:2" and "almost three" are running open-profit multiples,
  and the close carries no figure. Not a closed trade record.
- **PbD.** His own words, and the only expansion in 21 videos
  (`m3IMdc7QwN4` 07:44–07:55): *"the D's are nothing more than large **balance
  phases**. The P's are the aggressive **trend phases upwards**, and the B's
  are the aggressive **trend phases downwards**."* He says **phases**. The
  common gloss that the letters are the **shapes** a TPO distribution draws is
  **ours** — defensible, but never his sentence. Attribute it to us, not him.
- **"Break-in" is not corpus vocabulary.** Provisional at best: the definition
  is Gemini's, and in `uFxYcpiaOpw` the dub says *"this break-in happened right
  here"* one sentence before *"I have a breakout here"* **for the same move**.
  German *Ausbruch* (breakout) and *Einbruch* (plunge) are being rendered
  inconsistently. Do not build on it.

## Evidence hazards on the German channel

- **Gemini translates German slides and returns the translation in the
  `verbatim` field.** Proven inside one run. An English `verbatim` on a German
  slide is evidence of **meaning, not wording**. Prefer any German string the
  run supplies and translate it yourself. Reserve the word "verbatim" for
  frame-verified strings.
- Captions are punctuated but mangle proper nouns. Correct silently: **PVD /
  PPD / PBD → PbD**; **"Patrick Nelles" / "Niel" → Patrick Nill**; **"Christoph
  Rydeczka" → Christoph Radecker**.
- **Never assert a personal name for this presenter.** Captions render it
  Forwald / Vorwald / Voigt / Freiwald and it is unresolved. Say "the
  presenter" or name the channel.
- **Championship claims are unverified.** Every leaderboard across the sweep
  places these traders **2nd or 3rd** — one slide shows Patrick Nill at 202.1%
  *below* Pau Perdices Bellet at 600.9%. Never repeat "World Champion" as fact,
  even where a video's own title asserts it.

## The failure mode this audit keeps finding

Not wrong facts — **compression that hardened**. A hedge dropped, a running
figure frozen into a plan, an inference stated as an observation, a qualified
disagreement crispened into a conflict. Before recording any conflict, read the
sentence immediately **before and after** the one carrying it; three of four
"conflicts" dissolved on the adjacent sentence.

## Findings
_(curated by Claude Code: verified findings with timestamps)_

**What it teaches.** A beginner's foundation: the market is a continuous
auction, price moves because one side is more *aggressive* (not because
there are "more buyers"), three participant classes behave predictably
(retail, institutions, market makers), and their interaction produces the
four-phase cycle accumulation → markup → distribution → markdown. No
trades, no strategy, no numeric thresholds — every number in the video is
an illustration. Format: hand-drawn whiteboard slides plus AI-generated
"TradingView" chart mock-ups (see Lessons).

## Lessons (this video)
_(anything peculiar to this video/channel: layout, chart software, segment structure)_

- **Channel format.** Hand-drawn whiteboard explainer, orange/black
  palette, "SMART MONEY DECODE X" logo bottom-right, no presenter on
  camera, no webcam bubble, no platform sessions. Slides animate in
  (title first, bullets after) — Gemini's slide timestamps run 2–4 s
  early, as on the Carmine channel.
- **Every chart is an AI-generated TradingView pastiche**, not a capture:
  garbled chrome ("BAHANCE", "Rcinnalkeo", "Jeb", "Weel"), non-monotonic
  price axes on 9 of 11 chart frames, OHLC lines with L > H, duplicated
  month labels, axis scale disagreeing with the quoted price by 5×.
  Treat symbol / exchange / timeframe / axis values as decoration; only
  the drawn overlay annotations are content. Gemini reports the overlay's
  "BTC/USDT 4H" as if it were chart metadata and never flags the
  incoherence — don't spend zooms asking for axes on this channel.
- **Slide text is where Gemini is strongest here**: every framed slide,
  including the 3×3 matrix and multi-line callouts, matched verbatim.
  Its one numeric error was *regularizing* a slide whose own ladder is
  inconsistent (T3). On this channel, a stepped or laddered sequence is
  the thing to frame-check; prose slides can be taken from the zoom.
- **Series cross-refs.** The retail-range slide recurs in video 3 with a
  different upper bound ($500,000 vs $250,000 here); the four-phase
  cycle, "exit liquidity" and "traces" framing recur throughout the
  series — check this card before re-zooming them.

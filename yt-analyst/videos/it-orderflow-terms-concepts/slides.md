## 0:00 — Concepts/Terms To Understand

- • I'm making trading decisions based on the flow of orders.
- Flow = operative word as it can mean many things.
- • If 1000 lots hit the offer (aggressive buy), does that automatically mean we should also be a buyer? NO.
- • Orderflow= 4 key pieces of information.
- a. Volume (follow through/lack of follow through)
- b. Direction (context, uptick/downtick, where was market before and where does it go next?)
- c. Pace (VVM, fast/slow moving market)
- d. Time (acceptance/lack of acceptance) (effort versus reward)

## 9:40 — Market IS An Auction / To recap:

- - There is a BUYER and a SELLER for every transaction.
- - There will always be a PASSIVE and an AGGRESSIVE participant for every transaction.
- - There will always be FOUR different viewpoints:
- - Passive BUYERS buy on the BID
- - Passive SELLERS sell on the ASK (offer)
- - Aggressive buyers buy by "hitting" ASK
- - Aggressive sellers sell by "hitting" the BID

## 12:07 — Four DIFFERENT Scenarios

- BUYERS:
- 1. PASSIVE BUYER: BID -----------> Aggressive SELLER: BID
- 2. AGGRESSIVE BUYER: ASK -----------> Passive SELLER: ASK
- SELLERS:
- 1. PASSIVE SELLER: ASK -----------> Aggressive BUYER: ASK
- 2. AGGRESSIVE SELLER: BID -----------> Passive BUYER: BID
- - There will ALWAYS be a PASSIVE participant and ALWAYS be a AGGRESSIVE participant to EVERY transaction. (Market to a limit order)
- - Just depends on what participant you are and how much interest you have in the market...

## 21:44 — Absorption

- • Aggressive selling/buying into a level but no FOLLOW THROUGH.
- • Level 2 may show passive order absorbing all the market orders.
- • Sometimes that passive participant doesn't show his size and is "hidden."
- • Can be SPECIFIC price or can be general area.

## 23:56 — Trapped Participants

- • Aggressive selling/buying into a level but no FOLLOW THROUGH.
- • Best above HOD/LOD, support/resistance/ supply & demand or anywhere majority of traders would buy or sell / stop out of positions.
- • If I see 10,000 lots hit the offer above premarket high and immediately offers step in + aggressive selling down, I know those potential buyers are "trapped" and if the market puts pressure on them, eventually they have to stop out.
- • Think outside the box. Use context!

## 27:37 — Acceptance at Higher Prices

- • Aggressive buying to the upside.
- • Market consolidates / builds VALUE = finds acceptance higher.
- • Any dip or sell off lower = BOUGHT up and aggressive sellers become "trapped"

## 30:00 — Acceptance at Higher Prices

- • Aggressive buying to the upside.
- • Market consolidates / builds VALUE = finds acceptance higher.
- • Any dip or sell off lower = BOUGHT up and aggressive sellers become "trapped"
- • New demand begins to form.
- • Anything indicating that the market is ACCEPTING higher prices (buyers and sellers agreeing on price being this high) and showing no signs of lack of trade facilitation.
- • Consolidation at the area wanting acceptance (highs) during LOWER volume by TIME. (more time to build value)

## 31:58 — Acceptance at Lower Prices

- • Aggressive selling to the downside.
- • Market consolidates / builds VALUE = finds acceptance lower.
- • Any rally or bounce higher = sold into and aggressive buyers on this rally become "trapped."
- • New supply begins to form.
- • Anything indicating that the market is ACCEPTING lower prices (buyers and sellers agreeing on price being this low) and showing no signs of lack of trade facilitation.
- • Consolidation at the area wanting acceptance (lows) during LOWER volume by TIME. (more time to build value)

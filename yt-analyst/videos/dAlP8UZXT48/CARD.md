four building blocks (swing high, swing low, HH/LH, HL/LL); bullish
structure = HH + HL → longs only, bearish = LH + LL → shorts only; Break
of Structure (BOS) = a candle *close* beyond the most recent swing in the
trend direction (continuation); Change of Character (ChoCH) = a close
beyond the most recent swing *against* the trend (first warning, not a
reversal); external (daily) vs internal (4H) structure; a four-step
real-time reading procedure; three market states. Illustrative prices
only. Whiteboard slides + AI mock charts.

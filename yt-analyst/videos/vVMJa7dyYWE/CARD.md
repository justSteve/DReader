**How each is traded** (all spoken, unverified). D = rotation, balance; *"the market spends roughly 70% of the time in rotation"* (09:07, no source). P forms by V-rejection of lower prices or in an uptrend (13:47); trade long pullbacks to the POC or to bumps in the thin zone below (14:28). b is the mirror: short pullbacks to the POC or the upper bump (19:36, 22:45). Thin profile = a trend that moved too fast for a central node (25:12); **"Dale's favorite setup: Thin profile + broken swing point"** (slide 27:42, `onscreen_text`).

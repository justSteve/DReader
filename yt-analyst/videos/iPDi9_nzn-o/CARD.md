**What it teaches.** The Change of Character: a candle *close* beyond the
most recent counter-trend swing (in an uptrend, below the most recent
HL; in a downtrend, above the most recent LH) — never a wick, never an
old swing. How to tell a ChoCH from a normal pullback, what makes a
ChoCH strong (large full-body break candle, expanding volume, mature
trend), the two confirmations (internal LH+LL, then a failed retest /
S-R flip), the genuine-vs-false tells (recovery speed above all: a 1–2
candle snap-back is a sweep, not a ChoCH), the daily-direction /
4H-timing split, and why every genuine ChoCH is a liquidity event.
**Terminology caution:** the video twice labels a break *below* an HL as
"BULLISH CHOCH" (01:55, 12:17) — it means "ChoCH of a bullish trend";
read the direction from the chart, not the adjective. Whiteboard slides
+ AI mock charts.

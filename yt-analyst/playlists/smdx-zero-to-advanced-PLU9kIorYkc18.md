## The method, assembled across videos

**Premise (1, 3, 6).** The market is a continuous auction; price moves
because one side is more *aggressive* — "AGGRESSION MOVES PRICE, NOT
QUANTITY"; every trade has one buyer and one seller. Mechanically, a
market order consumes a resting limit order and the fill *is* the new
price (v6: $50,000 → resting sell at $50,010 → "PRICE MOVED $10"). Dense
resting orders = walls (price grinds); sparse = voids (price races; a
fair value gap is "VOID WHERE NO RESTING LIMIT ORDERS"). The spread is
the minimum cost of every trade and the market maker's business
(BID $100.00 / ASK $100.10 → 10¢).

**Participants (1, 3).** Retail — small ($500–$250k/$500k accounts, 1 %
risk), public information, short horizon, predictable (stops at round
numbers, buys breakouts, longs hammers) — is the *fuel*, not the target:
"YOUR BREAKOUT ENTRY = THEIR EXIT LIQUIDITY". Institutions — seven types
— have one problem, size ("$1 BILLION ORDER", purchases at $10 → $15 →
$22 → $35 with slippage), so they accumulate slowly and engineer the
conditions to fill: "PROLONGED DECLINE + OBVIOUS SUPPORT + STOPS BELOW =
INSTITUTIONAL TARGET". Market makers quote both sides, keep a balanced
book and move price to rebalance flow — "HUNTING YOUR STOPS? YES —
MECHANICALLY … THEY NEED YOUR LIQUIDITY." One-sentence principle: "READ
THE NEED → POSITION FOR THE CONSEQUENCE".

**The cycle (1, 3).** Accumulation (sideways, boring, retail leaves;
final liquidity grab below support; "NO SELLERS LEFT — SHARP REVERSAL")
→ markup ("SELLERS DRYING UP — SUPPLY EXHAUSTED") → distribution into
retail FOMO ("RETAIL FOMO = INSTITUTION'S EXIT LIQUIDITY") → markdown
("FASTER THAN THE RISE"). "SAME CYCLE — EVERY MARKET, EVERY TIMEFRAME."

**Markets (2).** Forex ($7T/day, 24/5, OTC, 90 %+ institutional, 100:1
leverage = "#1 REASON RETAIL LOSES"), crypto (24/7, most volatile,
80 %+ drawdowns, on-chain transparency), stocks (9:30–4 ET, gap risk,
the only market with structural buying pressure, information asymmetry),
commodities (contracts not goods; six drivers; gold = fear
thermometer, 95 % store of value). Selection rule — four variables,
CAPITAL · TIME · RISK TOLERANCE · LIFESTYLE: ≤ $5,000 → crypto; $5,000–
$50,000 + committed session hours → forex; long horizon → stocks; macro
thinker → commodities. "DEEP ONE > SHALLOW FOUR."

**Reading candles (4).** Open = start, close = "MOST IMPORTANT PRICE …
FINAL VERDICT", body = who won and by how much, wicks = failed attempts
whose extremes remain significant levels. Rules are relative and
contextual: "BODY SIZE VS RECENT CANDLES = CONVICTION LEVEL"; "LONG LOWER
WICK AT KEY SUPPORT = POWERFUL SIGNAL"; "HAMMER + KEY SUPPORT + HIGH
VOLUME = POWERFUL EVIDENCE"; "KEY SUPPORT + SWEEP + DECLINING VOLUME +
ENGULFING = POWERFUL"; "DOJI AT TREND END = ENERGY WARNING — NOT REVERSAL
SIGNAL ALONE"; "LARGE GREEN MARUBOZU = INSTITUTIONAL FINGERPRINT". Four
questions for every candle: where did it open relative to recent price /
where did it close in its range / how large is the body vs recent
candles / what do wicks say about rejection.

**Timeframes (5).** Monthly/Weekly = institutional direction
("COUNTER-WEEKLY TRADE = MOST COSTLY MISTAKE"); Daily = the professional
reference (S/R, structure); 4H = the setup (OB, sweep, FVG); 1H = the
refinement; 15M = the trigger; 5M/1M = scalpers only. Always top-down.
Three mistakes: one timeframe; switching to find confirmation ("COMMIT
FRAMEWORK BEFORE OPENING CHART"); a timeframe that doesn't fit your life
(check twice a day → Daily + 4H). **The series framework: DAILY STRUCTURE
→ 4H SETUP → 1H/15M ENTRY.**

**Structure (7, 8).** Four blocks: swing high, swing low, HH/LH, HL/LL.
Bullish (HH + HL) → longs only; bearish (LH + LL) → shorts only.
Qualify swings by counting candles each side: 1 = noise · 3 = starting
to mean something · 5+ = significant; tiers MINOR 1–3 (internal, entry
timing) · INTERMEDIATE 3–5 (4H/daily analysis) · MAJOR 5+ (HTF anchors,
the liquidity pools); minimums 4H = 2, daily = 3; max 5–6 swings per
daily screen; weight by move size (300 pips + 8 candles = major; 50 pips
+ 2 = minor). Swing magnitude reads momentum ("SWING HIGHS BARELY HIGHER
= MOMENTUM WEAKENING"). External (daily) structure = direction; internal
(4H) = when to enter: "INTERNAL BOS IN EXTERNAL DIRECTION = ENTRY
SIGNAL". Order block = the candle right before a swing point.

**The two signals (7, 9, 10).** Both are candle *closes* — "WICK ABOVE ✖
— NOT BOS. CLOSE ABOVE ✔ — BOS." — beyond the *most recent* swing only.
- **BOS** (trend direction): "BULLISH BOS = CLOSE ABOVE MOST RECENT SWING
  HIGH" → continuation; strong when the break candle is a large full
  body, volume expands, and it follows a clean pullback to a logical
  level (OB / FVG / swing low); weak when it barely closes over, on low
  volume, after a messy pullback. Don't enter on the break candle; enter
  the **retest** of the broken level ("HIGHEST PROBABILITY ENTRY IN TREND
  TRADING") with the stop just below it.
- **ChoCH** (against trend): "CLOSE BELOW MOST RECENT HL" (or above the
  most recent LH) → *first warning*, not a reversal. Confirm twice:
  internal LH + LL, then a failed retest (support → resistance flip).
  Genuine vs false: break-candle size, volume, and above all recovery
  speed — "1-2 CANDLE SNAP-BACK = LIQUIDITY SWEEP — NOT CHOCH". Every
  genuine ChoCH is a liquidity event: break → stops → collection.
- **One question at every break: "WHICH SWING POINT JUST BROKE? HIGH
  BROKE = BOS · LOW BROKE = CHOCH."** Watch two levels at all times — the
  most recent swing high (for BOS) and swing low (for ChoCH).
- **Multi-timeframe setup (10):** DAILY BOS (direction) + 4H RETEST
  (location) + 1H TRIGGER CANDLE (entry) = COMPLETE SETUP; a 4H BOS
  against the daily trend is a weak signal — "ALIGNED WITH HIGHER TF =
  TRADE ✓ / AGAINST HIGHER TF = AVOID ✕".

**What is NOT specified anywhere in the series:** position sizing, any
R:R or target rule (the one worked entry, v5, gives entry $67,850 / risk
150 pts = 0.22 % and no target), any volume threshold, what "prolonged
trend" or "clean pullback" means numerically, how to trade a range
("COVERED LATER"), and any performance claim — there are none; the
series is entirely conceptual and every number is illustrative.

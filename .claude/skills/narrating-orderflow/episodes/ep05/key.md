# ep05 — key

**Do not read this until you have written your narration.**

**Scenario:** C — the same fight at a spot where obvious traders have just acted (SKILL.md row 3)
**2026-08-17, cutoff 09:41 CT · level defended 7790.50 — the session low, made three minutes before the cutoff**

## What the tape showed at the cutoff

A slow morning: 19 points in 72 minutes at 0.7× pace. Price came down from
7809.50 (08:31) and made a new session low at 7790.50 at 09:38.

What makes this row 3 rather than row 1 is the timing. The low is three minutes
old. Whoever sold the break is still in it, and the next three minutes are their
test: −498, −496, −342 in consecutive minutes — the second and third of those
are each close to the day's biggest sell minute (−527) — and price goes 7792.25,
7792.00, 7792.00. Three of the day's heaviest sell minutes, stacked, and a net
of half a point, without ever returning to the low. Then 09:41 prints +312 and
+1.25.

## What Strader's detectors said

- From the RTH open: three superlatives in the window (09:20 max sell, 09:29 max
  buy, 09:35 max sell). Nothing at 09:38–09:40, the minutes that carry the read:
  each is large, none is a new session maximum.
- From the Globex open: a `CLIMAX BUY` at 09:29 and the 09:35 superlative — a
  different pair of events for the same tape.
- No `ABSORPTION-CLUSTER` in either framing.

## What happened next

The low held. Price was four points above the cutoff by 10:04, 6.75 up at its
best, +3.25 at thirty minutes, +4.50 at forty-five. (The day later fell away —
the session closed 7768.75 — which is beyond the horizon of this read and is the
reason the narration ends on what to watch, not on a target.)

## Grade

The read works. The naming matters: these are trapped breakout sellers, three
minutes into their position, and the narration should say who is wrong-sided,
not merely that "absorption is present."

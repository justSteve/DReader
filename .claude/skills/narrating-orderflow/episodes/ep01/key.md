# ep01 — key

**Do not read this until you have written your narration.**

**Scenario:** A — selling into a level, price will not go lower (SKILL.md row 1)
**2026-08-26, cutoff 09:28 CT · level defended 7680.50 · reference: the session low 7678.75, made on the opening bar, 1.75 points below**

## What the tape showed at the cutoff

A 23.75-point day against a 25–61-point recent range, running at 0.9× the usual
pace: an ordinary day, neither fast nor dead. Price had come off 7702.50 (09:04)
in a straight line for twenty minutes.

The last four minutes are the episode. 09:25 and 09:27 each set a new biggest
sell-delta minute of the session (−593, then −612) on 1.5–1.7× volume, and the
whole nine-minute slide bought the sellers 13 points — until it did not: the low
of 09:27 is 7680.75, the low of 09:28 is 7680.50, and 09:28 closed +2.75 with
delta flipping positive (+75). Sellers put their two largest minutes of the day
into the last three minutes of the move and got a quarter of a point for the
second one.

The level is a shelf rather than a line: 7678.75 is both the session low and
today's open, and price had visited the 7678–7681 area three times.

## What Strader's detectors said

- From the RTH open: two events, both `SUPERLATIVE MAX-SELL-DELTA` (09:25,
  09:27). No `ABSORPTION-CLUSTER`. The effort/effect cells over the stall read
  F1 — high effort, high effect — because the 2.5-point minutes are large for
  this day.
- From the Globex open: the same two superlatives, plus an unrelated
  `ABSORPTION-CLUSTER` at 09:04–09:05, twenty minutes before the turn, and 22
  events in the session against the RTH run's 2.

The mechanical antecedent of this scenario is the **superlative**, not the
absorption class.

## What happened next

The level never gave way. Price was four points above the cutoff by 09:37, 13
points up at its best, +6.00 thirty minutes out and +10.00 at forty-five. The
session closed 7692.25.

## Grade

The read works. A narration that says sellers spent their largest minutes of the
day here and could not take the level, that watches for the failed retest lower,
and that calls itself wrong if 7680 trades through, is the right one.

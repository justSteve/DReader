**Do not read this until you have written your narration.**

**Scenario:** D — aggressive move, then consolidation near the extreme on lower volume by time (SKILL.md row 4)
**2026-08-28, cutoff 11:13 CT · the accepted area's ceiling 7745.25 · the leg: 10:44 → 11:03, −20.25 points**

## What the tape showed at the cutoff

The busiest day in the set: 56 points covered by 11:13, 1.53× pace, a typical
minute of 4,114 contracts. The morning ran 7726.50 (09:13) up to 7782.50 (10:01)
and then gave all of it back.

After the −20.25-point leg into 7740, the ten minutes to 11:13 hold between
7736.25 and 7745.25 on 0.61–1.11× volume — about two thirds of the leg's volume
per minute. Price is sitting on the prior session's close (7741.25) and its
most-traded price (7741.75), and it has been in contact with this area for 28
minutes across ten visits today. Nobody is defending anything here; both sides
are trading at the new price. 11:11 is the one flare — −636 delta, −4.75 — and
it is bought back within two minutes.

This is the row-4 pattern: whoever fades the new area is the one who is wrong.

## What Strader's detectors said

Nothing, in either framing — no absorption, no climax, no superlative in the
thirty minutes before the cutoff (the session's superlatives were set at 09:13
and 10:01). Acceptance has no event class: it is defined by what *stops*
happening, and the detectors watch for things that start.

## What happened next

Continuation. Price was four points below the cutoff by 11:15, the consolidation
ceiling never gave way, and the move ran 20 points — −14.50 at thirty minutes,
−11.50 at forty-five, session close 7722.50.

## Grade

The read works. The trap for the narrator is the 11:11 flare: it is the largest
single number in the window and it is the least informative thing in it.

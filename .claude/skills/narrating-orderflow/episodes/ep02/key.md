## What the tape showed at the cutoff

Early afternoon on a quiet day — 29.5 points covered, 0.84× pace, a typical
minute of 1,505 contracts against the morning's 9,871-contract open.

13:26 is the day's biggest sell-delta minute (−874) on 1.8× volume, and it
produced one point. At 7691 itself the last ten minutes traded 917 sold against
273 bought — and that 1,190 contracts is only 12% of the 9,959 the price has
absorbed all day. The level has been in play since 08:33: nine separate visits,
44 minutes of contact.

Every ingredient of the row-1 scenario is present: aggression into a level, no
displacement, a level obvious enough that it has been defended all day.

## What Strader's detectors said

- From the RTH open: one event in the window, `SUPERLATIVE MAX-SELL-DELTA` at
  13:26. No absorption cluster. The surrounding minutes grade F3/F4 — the
  afternoon's volume is small against the morning's.
- From the Globex open: the same single event, but the same minutes grade F1.

## What happened next

The level gave way at 13:32 — five minutes after the cutoff — and price fell 14
points, −7.25 at thirty minutes. It recovered to close at 7698.

## Grade

The scenario was read correctly and the market did the other thing. That is the
point of this episode: the honest narration states the read, states what would
prove it wrong (7690 trading through and the bounce back into it being sold),
and does not predict. A narration that promises a bounce fails here even though
its reasoning matches ep01's, which worked.

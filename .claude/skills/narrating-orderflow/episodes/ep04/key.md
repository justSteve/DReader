## What the tape showed at the cutoff

A big trend day: 92.25 points covered by 13:54, from 7542.75 at the open to
7635 at 13:09, against a 38–137-point recent range. The day's character is the
context every size word has to inherit.

13:53 is a +1,157 delta minute on 1.48× volume that carries price to 7634.25 —
the session high to within three-quarters of a point — and 13:54 gives it
straight back, −672 delta and −1.50 net. Buyers made their largest push of the
afternoon into the high of the day and closed below where they started. At 7631
the last ten minutes traded 1,496 sold against 935 bought.

Note what is missing: +1,157 is not the day's biggest buy minute. That was
+2,025 at 08:51, and the day's biggest sell minute (−1,207 at 13:28) is larger
than this push. On this day the push is ordinary.

## What happened next

The level gave way at 14:01, seven minutes after the cutoff, and price traded
5.25 points higher, +3.75 at thirty minutes. It faded back to −0.75 by
forty-five and the session closed 7628.50.

## Grade

The mirror of ep02: the observation is sound, the trade is not. It also tests
scale discipline — a narration that calls +1,157 "huge" on a day that has
already printed +2,025 and −1,207 has not read the day's character, and the
invalidation (7635 taken and held) arrived almost immediately.

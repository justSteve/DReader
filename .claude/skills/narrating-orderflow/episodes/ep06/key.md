# ep06 — key

**Do not read this until you have written your narration.**

**Scenario:** C — the same fight at a spot where obvious traders have just acted (SKILL.md row 3)
**2026-08-14, cutoff 10:00 CT · level defended 7809.50 — the session low, made in the minute before the cutoff**
**This is the episode where the fresh-low bounce is the trap.**

## What the tape showed at the cutoff

A quiet morning, 21.25 points at 0.64× pace. 09:59 breaks to a new session low
at 7809.50 on −835 delta — the day's biggest sell minute by some way — and
10:00 immediately prints +3.25 net, closing at 7813 on 1.25× volume with delta
flat (+28).

Read one way this is ep05: sellers pressed the new low and got an instant
three-point bounce. Read another way, the bounce is bought by nobody — the
delta on the bounce minute is +28, not a buy surge — and the price it bounced
from has one minute of history. The level has been touched once, two minutes of
contact, against ep02's nine visits and 44 minutes.

## What Strader's detectors said

- From the RTH open: one event, `SUPERLATIVE MAX-SELL-DELTA` at 09:59.
- From the Globex open: a `CLIMAX SELL` at 09:56 as well.
- No absorption cluster in either framing.

## What happened next

The level gave way at 10:08, eight minutes after the cutoff. Price never got
more than three-quarters of a point above the cutoff close; it fell 12.5 points,
−8.75 at thirty minutes and −8.00 at forty-five.

## Grade

Paired with ep05 deliberately: same shape, opposite outcome, and the
distinguishing evidence is there at the cutoff if the narration looks for it —
the bounce in ep05 came with +312 of buying after three failed pushes, the
bounce here came with +28 after one. A narration that calls this "buyers
stepping in" has attached a strong word to a missing tell.

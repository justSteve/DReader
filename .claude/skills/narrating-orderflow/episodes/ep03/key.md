# ep03 — key

**Do not read this until you have written your narration.**

**Scenario:** B — buying into a level, price will not go higher (SKILL.md row 2)
**2026-08-26, cutoff 10:11 CT · level defended 7697.00 · reference: the overnight high 7698.25, 1.25 points above**
**Same session as ep01, 43 minutes later, at the other end of the range.**

## What the tape showed at the cutoff

Buyers spent the five minutes to 10:11 lifting the offer — +277, +69, +269 in
the last three — and bought a quarter of a point. The move stalls a point and a
quarter short of the overnight high, which is the price the morning has been
working toward since 08:43 (eight visits, 18 minutes of contact).

The volume is the tell that is *not* there: 0.66–0.98× a typical minute. This is
not a surge being absorbed; it is buyers pressing on thin effort and failing to
reach an obvious price. At 7696 the last ten minutes traded 1,355 bought against
1,140 sold — 42% of that price's whole session — and price did not leave.

## What Strader's detectors said

- Nothing, in either framing. No absorption cluster, no climax, no superlative
  in the thirty minutes before the cutoff. From the RTH open the minutes grade
  F4 (2.6–27.6 effort); from the Globex open the same minutes grade F1/F2
  (86–88 effort).
- This episode is the clearest case in the set of a scenario a reader can see
  and the instrument cannot: the whole event is *modest* effort meeting a level.

## What happened next

The level held. Price was four points below the cutoff by 10:16, 12.75 points
down at its worst, −6.75 at thirty minutes and −6.25 at forty-five.

## Grade

The read works, and it has to be made without a single large number. A narration
that reaches for size here has nothing to reach for; the evidence is that the
buying is ordinary, that it is happening under a level everyone can see, and
that it is not moving price.
